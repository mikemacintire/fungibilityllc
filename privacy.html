In short

This website is an informational page about our company. It has no accounts, no sign-up forms, and no advertising. We do not set cookies, we do not track you across websites, and we do not sell or share personal information. Our individual products collect data in order to function, and each is governed by its own separate privacy policy.

1Scope of this policy

This policy describes how Fungibility LLC ("Fungibility," "we," or "us") handles information in connection with the website at fungibilityllc.com.

It does not cover our products. Vanometry, Powerbuyer, and BiziEdit are separate applications that collect and process data in order to operate, and each publishes its own privacy policy on its own website. If you are a user of one of those products, that product's policy governs your data, not this one.

2Information we collect

This website does not ask you for personal information. There is no account system, no contact form, no newsletter signup, and no comment section. The only way to send us information through this site is to choose to email or call us using the contact details we publish.

Two categories of information arise automatically from ordinary web hosting:

  • Server logs. Like effectively all web servers, our hosting provider records basic technical details of each request: IP address, browser user agent, the page requested, and a timestamp. These logs exist for security and reliability purposes and are retained for a limited period according to our provider's standard practice.
  • Correspondence. If you email or call us, we keep that correspondence so that we can respond to you and maintain a record of the conversation.

3Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies of its own. It contains no advertising pixels, no cross-site tracking scripts, and no third-party behavioral analytics.

4Third-party services

This website loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those font files, Google receives your IP address and user agent as a normal part of serving the request. Google's handling of that information is governed by its own privacy policy, and we have no access to it.

The site is served by a third-party web hosting provider, which processes requests and maintains the server logs described above on our behalf.

Links on this site point to our own product websites and may point to other external sites. Following such a link takes you to a service we do not control under this policy.

We use the limited information described above only to operate and secure this website and to respond to people who contact us. We do not use it to build advertising profiles.
